Leap Clash

A browser-based multiplayer platform battle built with Node.js, Express.js, Socket.io, and Phaser.js. Players compete in real-time 1v1 matches as procedurally generated platforms shift beneath them, forcing quick reactions and skillful timing. The game features multiple platform variations with unique behaviors, creating a fast, dynamic, and replayable experience.
